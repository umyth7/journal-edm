Our Story

About Us

EDM Journal began its journey in 2012 as a passion project rooted in the underground electronic music scene. What started as a small community of dedicated music lovers has grown into one of the most recognisable independent electronic music media brands in the region.

Over the years, we have dedicated ourselves to showcasing the artists, events, and sounds that define the cutting edge of melodic house and techno — from intimate underground venues to the world's largest stages.

Today, EDM Journal reaches over 5 million people every month across all platforms, with a loyal and highly engaged audience that lives and breathes electronic music culture. Our content spans artist features, live sets, event coverage, and curated mixes that continue to resonate with fans worldwide.

We are independent, passionate, and uncompromising — built by music lovers, for music lovers.
